FORTFALL

Sling. Smash. Topple the fort.

Twelve physics puzzles. Load a stone, a bomb or a splitter, drag the pouch and let fly — break the walls, drop the roofs and squash every creature inside. Tapping mid-flight triggers the loaded shot's trick.

Choose a fort

How to play

  • Drag the pouch back and release to fire. The dotted arc shows where it will fly.
  • Tap (or press Space) while a shot is in the air to trigger it: bombs detonate, splitters break into three.
  • Kill every creature. Fewer shots used and more fort destroyed means a better score — and more stars.
  • Stone shrugs off hits, wood splinters, ice shatters. Bombs cut through anything nearby.
  • Thin walls fall over; heavy roofs squash whatever is underneath.
  • Keyboard: ←→ angle, ↑↓ power, Space fire, R restart, M mute.
